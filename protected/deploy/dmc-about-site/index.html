Our story

Built for the woman who opens her stall before the sun does.

Every morning across our markets, women arrive early to lay out tomatoes, fold wrappers, stack yams, and open shop. What slows most of them down isn't effort — it's a gap in working capital that the usual channels were never built to close. Banks want collateral and paperwork. Loan sharks want fear and silence.

DMC Sparkle Services exists to do it differently: we lend directly, we collect the way the market already runs — a small contribution each day — and we treat every woman who walks in as someone worth backing, whether this is her first loan or her fifth.
